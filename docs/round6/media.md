# Round-6 media: the owner's audio, the two videos, the ROSW route

What 6N sources and masters, what 6L and 6K load, and what the owner still has to tell us. Nothing here is a game
rule; the budgets are `docs/ASSETS.md` 6.8 (6K writes the round-6 text) and the music mapping is
`packages/content/data/music.json` (6L).

**Every sound the game ships comes from the ROSW sound library** (owner, 2026-10-22: "for the music, SFX and
more use the pipeline's new SFX skill"). ROSW `sound-library/` catalogues the owner's own recordings
(`catalog.json`, versioned; the WAVs on GitHub Releases of that repo, never in git), and its `sound-library`
skill searches the catalog, puts a file on disk by id and ingests new batches. The owner's seven music pieces
and four field recordings enter the library as a batch (section 1); the stingers, the narration bed and the
ambiences are picked from the 2,648 sounds already there (section 4); only the two videos go through a studio
run (section 3). `music-cue` is not used in round 6, and nothing is copied by hand into `studio/assets/`.

## 2. The music budget (DECISIONS A81)

Mastered as above the seven music files come to roughly 9 to 10 MB, above the 6 MB audio budget of ASSETS 6.8.
The decision: **music is lazy-loaded per map after Start** (never part of the first load) **and has its own
budget: at most 12 MB in all and at most 4 MB per map**; the 6 MB budget stays for SFX and ambience, which the
first load carries. `tools/sprites/test/media.test.ts` (6K) enforces both from `audio/manifest.json` and
`audio/music/manifest.json`, and the per-video cap of section 3.

**Amended 2026-10-23 (A98).** The `lobby` and `trailer` playlists play before any map loads, so they are a pre-match
bucket: counted toward the 12 MB total, never toward a map's 4 MB. The per-map cap is `media.ts`'s 4 MiB, and
`MusicDirector.test.ts` reads the same constant (6P). With the owner's theme whole in `lobby` (about 2.7 MB at q3)
all music comes to about 10.7 MB.

## 5. The ROSW side

In the ROSW checkout (`groot99-droid/Pipelines-Plugin`): the `sound-library` skill is read first and does the
searching, fetching and ingesting (`python sound-library/library.py search "<words>"`, `fetch --id <id>...`,
`ingest --from-release <tag>`); the fetched file lands at `sound-library/files/<category>/<slug>.<ext>`, is
**copied** (never moved) into 6N's working folder for mastering, and nothing under `files/` is ever committed.
`catalog.json` is never edited by hand; `ingest` is the only way in, and a `refused` line (a name already there
with different content) is asked about before `--replace`. For the two videos `studio/vault/CLAUDE.md` governs
the `video-shot` runs: a master is kept only through `studio_run.py keep` after the execute go-ahead, and a note
is written only by the pipeline. 6N works in both repos on one branch name (`claude/r6-rosw-media`): its ROSW PR
holds the catalog commit and the video runs' notes and catalog entries; its Who-Did-It PR holds binaries and
manifest rows only (no `.ts`, nothing under `packages/`), so it can merge whenever `media.test.ts` passes.

## 7. The provenance rows

`assets/source/MANIFEST.md` keeps one row per master. For a library sound the **Path** is the ROSW path the
catalog gives (`sound-library/files/<category>/<slug>.<ext>`), the **SHA-256** is the catalog's `sha256` (the
original's, not the OGG's), the **Studio note** reads "– (sound library)", the **Exports** are the OGG paths and
the **Source** reads "owner recording, ROSW `sound-library/catalog.json` `<id>`, release `<tag>`" (for the three
AI tracks: "AI-generated by the owner with <tool>, <date>; ROSW `sound-library/catalog.json` `<id>`"). A video
keeps the round-5 shape (a `studio/assets/who-did-it/video/<slug>/` master, a studio note, a Higgsfield catalog
id). `CREDITS.md` gets one row per shipped file, citing the same library id, as a Higgsfield row cites its
`studio/library/catalog.json` id.
