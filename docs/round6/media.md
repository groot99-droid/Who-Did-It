What 6N makes and masters, what 6L and 6K load, and what the owner still has to tell us. Nothing here is a game
rule; the budgets are `docs/ASSETS.md` 6.8 (6K writes the round-6 text) and the music mapping is
`packages/content/data/music.json` (6L).

**Provenance.** The three OuterSkylight tracks are AI-generated by the owner; `docs/ASSETS.md` 6.5 logs an AI
output with its tool, feature and date, and the owner names the tool to 6N before the CREDITS rows are written.
The GarageBand pieces and the four field recordings are the owner's originals and carry CC BY 4.0 like every
owner original (DECISIONS A19). Each of the eleven gets one `CREDITS.md` row, one `assets/source/MANIFEST.md` row
(master path in ROSW, SHA-256, date) and one ROSW studio note written by the `studio-pipeline` (never by hand).

**Mastering** (6N, in ROSW). Music to OGG Vorbis q5 at -18 LUFS integrated, SFX to OGG q5 at -16 LUFS, peaks at
-1 dBTP, a 20 ms fade at both ends of every loop point, and every music track trimmed to a clean loop (the day
themes loop; the beds loop; the trailer track does not). The mastered files are what lands under
`apps/client/public/assets/audio/` and nothing else does: no `.mp3`, `.m4a` or `.wav` is committed.

## 2. The music budget (DECISIONS A81)

Mastered as above the seven music files come to roughly 9 to 10 MB, above the 6 MB audio budget of ASSETS 6.8.
The decision: **music is lazy-loaded per map after Start** (never part of the first load) **and has its own
budget: at most 12 MB in all and at most 4 MB per map**; the 6 MB budget stays for SFX and ambience, which the
first load carries. `tools/sprites/test/media.test.ts` (6K) enforces both from `audio/manifest.json` and
`audio/music/manifest.json`, and the per-video cap of section 3.

## 4. Stingers and beds (6N, ROSW `music-cue`; 6L, CC0)

- **ROSW `music-cue`** makes the short cues the owner's tracks do not cover: `sting_kill` (2 s), `sting_meeting`
  (3 s), `sting_eject` (3 s), `sting_win_village`, `sting_win_cabal` (5 s each), `narr_bed_hollowmere` (the
  opening's 40 s narration bed). They are SFX for the budget (`audio/manifest.json`, the `sting_*` and `narr_*`
  keys 6L adds to `AUDIO_KEYS`) and go through the same master, note, MANIFEST and CREDITS route.
- **CC0 beds** (6L, from the sources `docs/ASSETS.md` 6.5 allows) fill the playlist slots the owner's seven
  tracks leave empty on the two round-6 maps: `meeting`, `finalNight`, `gameOver` and the ONUB `night` class.
  A CC0 row needs no attribution but gets a CREDITS row anyway, with the source URL and the download date.

## 5. The ROSW side

In the ROSW checkout (`groot99-droid/Pipelines-Plugin`): `studio/vault/CLAUDE.md` governs every run; the
`render-pipeline` skill's "Kinds" line names this document for the `audio` and `video` kinds; a master is kept
only through `studio_run.py keep` after the execute go-ahead; a note is written only by the pipeline. 6N works
in both repos on one branch name (`claude/r6-rosw-media`), and its Who-Did-It PR holds binaries and manifest
rows only (no `.ts`, nothing under `packages/`), so it can merge whenever `media.test.ts` passes.
