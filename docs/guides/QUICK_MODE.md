# Quick mode: One Night Ultimate Baguette

A short round for the same party: no walking, one night, one argument, one vote. The leader switches
the mode chip in the lobby settings to **One Night Ultimate Baguette**. Timers, decks, stations and the
winners table are in [`docs/GDD.md`](../GDD.md) 3.11.

## The fiction

Paris, the graveyard shift before the Bake-Off. You are bakers keeping the Mother Dough alive through
the night. Hidden among you are Team Mold, who want it ruined. Nobody leaves the kitchen and nobody dies
in the night: at dawn you argue, then one of you goes into the brick oven.

## Two cards each

- Your **Role card** says who you were dealt.
- Your **Location card** is the prep station you choose to work at.
- The **Pantry** in the middle of the table holds a few spare Role and Location cards.

Cards move during the night. **You play for the team of the Role card in front of you at dawn**, not the
one you were dealt. After the night nobody may look at their own cards, so the screen shows only what you
remember: what you were dealt, where you worked, and what your own night act showed you.

## The phases

1. **Role card.** Read it. Press Ready. Nobody sees who is ready.
2. **Evening Prep.** Pick a station, play its mini-game, and play one Ingredient card into its pile.
   Prep ends early when everyone has committed.
3. **The crime scenes.** Every pile is shuffled and flipped. Flags, net weight and any ruin are
   announced to all.
4. **The Baking Cycle.** The roles wake one by one in the order the deck sets and tamper with the cards:
   peek, swap, move a Pantry card. When it is your turn you get a short window and a screen to act on.
5. **Dawn.** The Iron Cloche is shown.
6. **Morning Rush.** Everyone argues. Claims are structured sentences you tap together. You can cast and
   change a vote the whole time.
7. **"Un, deux, trois, CRUST!"** The last seconds to point.
8. **The Final Bake.** Who goes in the oven, and what card they held.
9. **Game over.** Every card, pile and night act is shown. Back to the lobby with the same code.

## Winning

- **Loyal Bakers** win when at least one Mold Spore goes in the oven. If every Spore sits in the Pantry,
  they win by leaving the oven empty or baking a Team Mold card.
- **Team Mold** wins when the Mother Dough is ruined at prep, or when no Spore goes in the oven.
- **Le Croissant Rassis** plays alone and wins by going in the oven.

## Tips

- Say what you saw at night, but remember your card may have moved after you looked.
- Watch the prep reveal: a ruined pile points at whoever worked that station.
- A player who claims a role nobody else contests is probably telling the truth. Two players claiming
  the same role cannot both be.
