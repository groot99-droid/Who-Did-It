# Feedback form

The form every player fills after a session, before the debrief (ROADMAP 7.5). Build it once in
Google Forms (Blank form; Settings > Responses: don't collect email addresses; Presentation: show
the progress bar) and reuse it for every session by changing the date in the description. Each
item below is one question: the title as written, the type in brackets, then the options. Items
1 to 8 are the ROADMAP 7.5 list; nothing else is scored. Turn on "Required" where it says so.

**Form title:** Who Did It, playtest feedback

**Form description:** Thanks for playing. Answer for the whole session, not one match. Two minutes;
please fill it in before we talk about the game. Session: {date}, {milestone}.

---

**0. Your name** [Short answer] Optional. Only the host reads it, to match your answers with the
notes; the notes keep a summary, never names.

**1. Which roles did you play?** [Checkboxes] Required.
- Killer
- Detective
- Doctor
- Townsfolk
- Ghost for part of a match (I was killed or voted out)

**2. "I understood my role within 20 seconds of seeing the card."** [Linear scale] Required.
1 to 5, label 1 "Strongly disagree", label 5 "Strongly agree".

**3. "I knew where to go."** [Linear scale] Required.
1 to 5, label 1 "Strongly disagree", label 5 "Strongly agree".

**4. "The vote felt fair."** [Linear scale] Required.
1 to 5, label 1 "Strongly disagree", label 5 "Strongly agree".
Description: Fair means you had something to go on, not that your side won.

**5. What was the most confusing moment?** [Paragraph] Required.

**6. What did you play on?** [Multiple choice] Required.
- Phone
- Tablet
- Laptop or desktop
- Other: (the "Other" option)

Add a second question in the same section: **6b. Which device and browser?** [Short answer]
Optional. Example: "Pixel 7, Chrome" or "MacBook, Safari".

**7. How likely are you to play again?** [Linear scale] Required.
1 to 10, label 1 "Not at all", label 10 "Tonight, if you'll have me".

**8a. One thing to add.** [Short answer] Required.

**8b. One thing to remove.** [Short answer] Required. Description: "Nothing" is a fine answer.

---

**Confirmation message:** Thanks. Don't tell the others your answers until the debrief.

## Reading the responses

Responses > the Sheets icon makes a sheet. Per session the scorecard takes: the mean of 2, 3 and 4;
the full list of 7 (every answer, not only the mean, since a few low scores matter); a one-line
theme per answer to 5; and the 8a and 8b answers grouped where they repeat. 1 and 6 are context
for the other answers: a low 2 from someone who played the Killer reads differently from the same
score from a Townsfolk player.
