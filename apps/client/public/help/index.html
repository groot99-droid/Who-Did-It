Who Did It: help

A hidden-role whodunit for four to ten friends. Most of you are villagers; a few are the Cabal, who kill after dark and lie about it. Find the bodies, read the clues, argue, and vote.

Quick start

  1. One player presses Create Party and shares the invite link or the party code.
  2. Everyone else opens the link (or presses Join Party and types the code), then presses Ready.
  3. The leader presses Start. Each player gets a secret role card. Read it; Tab reopens it.
  4. By day, explore, gather and compare notes. After dark, stay in pairs, sneak, and listen for screams.
  5. Report bodies with E. In a meeting, present a Notebook entry and vote. Then a new day.

Controls

Move WASD or the arrow keys; drag on touch
Sneak hold Shift (quiet, slower)
Run Space (louder)
Use E: doors, the bell, report a body; hold on a glowing clue to collect it
Ability Q, R or F, as your card shows
Role card / Notebook Tab / N
Skip a cutscene Esc or the Skip button

Something stuck?

  • "Waking the village...": the server is starting. Wait a few seconds.
  • No sound: click Create or Join once (browsers block audio until then), then check the gear for mute.
  • Old page after an update: hard-reload with Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R.
  • Party not found: codes never use 0, O, 1 or I; a started party is locked.
