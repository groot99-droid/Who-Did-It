# tools/blender

The V1 render rig (`docs/ASSETS.md` 6.2, `docs/SPRITES.md`). Python only; it runs inside Blender
5.2 LTS or under the `bpy` 5.2 wheel. Nothing here is committed as a `.blend`: `build_rig.py`
rebuilds `render_rig.blend` from code, and `batch_render.py` builds it in memory when no `--rig` is given.

|---|---|

## Room checks

What a first-person room (`meshy_room_build.py`) must show before it ships as a walk file of its own: the walk viewer's
contract, the collision, and what the baked light displays. These are the scripts the widow's bedroom was judged
ready with on 2026-11-16 by its independent check (a review of the finished room by someone other than its builder;
that check's record is not in the repo), unchanged in what they measure. They read the build and never write it;
`run.sh` takes the GLB's md5 before and after. The names: `cc_` for the contract and collision checks, `r7_` and
`r10_` for the rounds of the bedroom's work that added them (7 and 10). What the checks know of a room comes from the
builder's manifest and the scene (G2b, `roomcfg.Facts`): the bounds and the ceiling, every door and window with its
wall and span, every spawn and portal as placed, the lights the room exports, the ids that carry no COLOR_0 on purpose,
what each scene spot and prop stands for (`coverage`) and which pieces hang (`rise`). "The room's check file" is
`room_checks/rooms/<roomId>.json`, optional: the judged failures (`known_fails`), the browser's sweep points, a size
window of the room's own, more banned words, and the numbers of a manifest written before G2b (no `openings`), which it
stands in for. A room with one door and one window keeps the bedroom's check names; a room with more has one check per
opening, `<name>:<opening id>`. "The room file" is `meshy_rooms/<roomId>.json`.

A finished room must also stay as it was judged. **After every change to shared builder code, run `regress.sh`** for
every room that has a golden row (the last rows of the table): it rebuilds the room from its saved bake and compares
the GLB with the room's golden md5 and the manifest's numbers with the row's digest. It cannot see what Cycles would
compute (the rebuild skips the bake): a change to the bake rig or the groups is proven with `regress_bake.sh`.

- **Results** go to `tools/blender/build/rooms/checks/<roomId>/` (`<roomId>_quick/` for a quick run; `--out` moves it): each
  check's `.json` and `.log`, `cc_reach.png`, `cc_grid.npz`, `cc_vertices.npz` (7 MB), `glb.md5`, `glb_after.md5`,
  `steps.tsv` and `summary.json`; the pages' results to `browser/` under it. The paths in the `.json` results and the
  two `.md5` files are from the repo root; a file or an `--out` folder outside the repo keeps its full path. The `.log`
  files hold absolute local paths (Blender prints the `.blend` it reads, Python the script behind a warning or an
  error, `bl.sh` the lock it waits for): do not publish them as they are. The gate's rebuild goes to
  `tools/blender/build/rooms/regress/<roomId>/` (`--out` moves it; two gates on one room at the same time each need
  their own): the room's `.glb`, `.blend`, `.json` and textures, `build.log` (the builder's output, with the same
  absolute paths) and `regress.json` (the verdict).
- **The build and the saved bake are local files.** `build/` is git-ignored: `build/rooms/<id>.glb`, `.blend`, `.json`,
  the stills and `<id>_raw_groups.npz` (the saved bake, from which `meshy_room_build.py --raw` rebuilds the room in
  about a minute without baking) exist only on the machine that built them. A fresh clone and CI have no room to
  check, and a lost saved bake means baking again (the command above). The gate needs the saved bake too:
  without it `regress.sh` ends with exit code 3 and compares nothing. Two bakes of one build gave the same light and
  the same GLB at 1 sample a group (2026-11-16), and so did the rebuild from the first one's saved bake; two more did
  on 2026-11-21, array for array with the AO (`regress_bake.sh --record`); a second bake at the bedroom's 384 samples
  has not been tried.
- **What they need.** Git Bash on Windows for the `.sh` files (`bl.sh` asks Windows' `tasklist` whether a Blender
  runs); a `python` with numpy and Pillow, or `PYTHON` naming another (`regress.py` needs the standard library only);
  the Windows build of Blender 5.2 for `cc_geom.py`, `cc_cracks.py` and `r7_extra.py`, which load its glTF add-on's
  Draco bridge (`bf_intern_draco_bridge.dll`) and so run on no other build. Relative paths: `run.sh` and
  `regress.sh` take them from where you stand; `bl.sh` changes to the repo root before Blender starts, so in a check
  run alone through it a relative `--out` is taken from the root.
- **Passed** means the full run's last line is `PASS` or `FAIL: N failed (N known, 0 new)`, each followed only by
  `-> <out>/summary.json`: no new fail, no script that did not run, the GLB unchanged (`summary.json`: `fails_new`
  and `did_not_run` empty, `glb_unchanged` true). `regress.py record` is for a room in that state. A quick run is
  never a pass: the bedroom's ends with `FAIL: 3 failed (2 known, 1 new)`, the new one
  `cc_geom:walls_closed_collision_fans`.

## Character file contract

- A collection named `Export` holding everything to render.
- Mesh parts carry the custom property `wdi_part`: `body`, `scarf`, `hat_cap`, `hat_bowler` or
  `blood` (a slightly inflated torso and hands shell). Overlay passes show one part and turn the body
  into a holdout, so overlays are occluded exactly as on the body.
- An empty named `Feet` on the ground between the feet; it may follow the root bone.
- One action per clip, named `idle`, `walk`, `swing`, `die`, at a scene rate that is a whole multiple
  of 12 fps (24 by default). Looping clips (`idle`, `walk`) end on a copy of their first pose, which
  is not rendered; one-shot clips render their last frame.
- `manifest.json` gets the `Feet` pivot from every pass, so a run of overlay passes alone still writes
  complete entries. Runs merge into an existing `manifest.json` under a file lock, so a set split by
  `--anims`, `--dirs` or `--passes`, sequential or in parallel into one `--out`, adds up to one
  manifest; a body-pass run replaces a frame's entry (and its `clipped` flag), an overlay-only run only
  updates the pivot.
- The renderer palettizes materials, adds the `Outline` hull to every part except `blood`, and warns
  when a clip does not sample to the V1 frame count or a body frame touches the cell edge.

## Calibration results and rig decisions (2026-10-10, SpriteCam, retired in round 5)

- `Feet` at the origin projects to (64, 112) in a 128 px cell; the 1 m cube is 93 px tall
  (90 px plus the outline). The smoke sheet lifts the frame 24 px so a cube turned 45° fits.
- Key light azimuth is **225°** (camera side, screen left), not 315°: at 315° the key sat behind the
  subject and every camera-facing surface fell into the shadow band. Recorded in `ASSETS.md` 6.2.
- The fill's "strength 0.6" is read as the irradiance of a 0.6 sun: 220 W at 10 m measured, so the
  fill uses `3.67 W x d²` at 30 m and 12 m wide, with a custom cutoff distance (EEVEE's light
  threshold culled it otherwise). Far and wide keeps it even across a sprite; a near area light put
  ramp-stop splits across flat faces.
- The key sun has `angle = 0`; the checker texture is offset 1 mm because the cube's faces sat on its
  square boundaries and TAA flipped between colours.
- Toon ramp stops are **0.30 / 0.70**, not 0.42 / 0.78. The ramp sees the luma of the lit diffuse:
  about 0.87 x N·L of the key (3/π for a strength-3 sun times the key colour's luma) plus a little
  fill. Measured on the rig: camera-facing faces 0.35, the lit diagonal side 0.49, the far side 0.02,
  top faces and the ground 0.77. At 0.42 / 0.78 the front faces sat in the shadow band and the tops
  and ground in the mid band (the ground read flat slate); the new stops leave at least 0.05 margin.
- `die` renders 256 px cells with the feet at the centre (`rig_common.CLIP_CELL`): a 1.7 m body
  falling towards the camera spans about 90 px below the feet, which no 128 px cell can hold. The smoke sheet renders a fallen 1.7 m proxy in that cell: feet at
  (128, 128), the body reaching 85 px below them.
